An open-source PyTorch-style library for zeroth-order optimization, enabling memory-efficient fine-tuning with up to **50% memory reduction**. Implements SignSGD, Muon, and all novel momentum-based ZO methods, exposed through a `torch.optim`-style API.

- **GitHub**: [github.com/modernTalker/zero-order-optimization](https://github.com/modernTalker/zero-order-optimization/tree/dev)
- **Related paper**: [Leveraging Coordinate Momentum in SignSGD and Muon (ICML Workshop on Tiny Titans, 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04430)
